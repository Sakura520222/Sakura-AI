Refund Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

1. Overview

We want you to be satisfied with Sakura AI Reviewer. This Refund Policy explains when and how we issue refunds for our paid plans.

2. Subscription Refunds

Refund Window

  • Monthly Plans: Full refund available within 7 days of purchase if fewer than 50 AI review requests have been used.
  • Annual Plans: Full refund available within 14 days of purchase if fewer than 200 AI review requests have been used.

3. When Refunds Apply

We will process a refund under the following conditions:

  • Service outage exceeding 48 consecutive hours during your billing period.
  • Significant degradation of core features (AI review, issue analysis) for more than 72 hours within a billing period.
  • Accidental purchase or duplicate charges (with proof).
  • Unauthorized charges (reported promptly upon discovery).

4. When Refunds Do NOT Apply

  • Usage exceeding the applicable refund window thresholds.
  • Dissatisfaction with AI review quality (as AI-generated suggestions are advisory and should be reviewed by the user).
  • Changes to features or pricing with at least 30 days advance notice.
  • Account suspension or termination due to violation of our Terms of Service.
  • Failure to cancel a recurring subscription before renewal.

5. How to Request a Refund

  1. Send an email to support@firefly520.top with the subject "Refund Request".
  2. Include your account identifier (GitHub username or Telegram ID) and order number.
  3. Briefly describe the reason for the refund request.
  4. We will review your request within 3 business days and process approved refunds within 7 business days.

6. Refund Method

Refunds are processed through our payment processor (Paddle) and will be credited back to the original payment method. The timing of the refund appearing on your statement depends on your payment provider (typically 5-10 business days).

7. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time through the billing settings in your account. Upon cancellation:

  • Your plan benefits remain active until the end of the current billing period.
  • No further charges will be made after cancellation.
  • Cancellation is not the same as a refund — please submit a separate refund request if applicable.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification. Please review this page periodically for updates.

9. Contact

For refund inquiries, contact us at support@firefly520.top.
